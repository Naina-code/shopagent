# Decision Log

## Decision 1
Considered:
Traditional filters and product grids

Chosen:
Conversational AI flow

Because:
Natural language shopping is more intuitive and reduces decision fatigue.

---

## Decision 2
Considered:
Using external LLM APIs

Chosen:
Rule-based recommendation engine

Because:
Lower cost, easier deployment, faster testing, and no API dependency.

---

## Decision 3
Considered:
Backend-heavy architecture

Chosen:
Frontend-first architecture

Because:
Faster prototyping and easier demo deployment.

---

## Decision 4
Considered:
Simple recommendations only

Chosen:
Tradeoff-aware recommendations

Because:
Users need explanations, not just product lists.

---

## Decision 5
Considered:
Single-turn interaction

Chosen:
Multi-turn conversational memory

Because:
Shopping decisions require iterative refinement.
